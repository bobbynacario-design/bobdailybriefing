Tow truck fees for light vehicles

Last updated:
01 July 2026
Official information by:

These fees are valid for the 2026 to 2027 period. The listed charges exclude any applicable GST.  This summary document is provided as a quick reference guide only. For full details please refer to Part 5 of the Tow Truck Regulation 2020.
