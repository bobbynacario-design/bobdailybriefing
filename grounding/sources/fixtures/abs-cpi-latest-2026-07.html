Key statistics

In the 12 months to July 2026:

  • The Consumer Price Index (CPI) rose 3.5%, down from 3.8% in the 12 months to June 2026.
  • The largest contributors to annual inflation were Housing (+5.0%), Food and non-alcoholic beverages (+3.2%) and Recreation and culture (+2.6%).
  • Trimmed mean inflation was 3.6%, unchanged from 3.6% in the 12 months to June 2026.

In the month of July, the CPI rose 1.0% in original and 0.6% in seasonally adjusted terms.
